# Wizard Rumble 0.9.2 playtest guide

This guide covers 0.9.2's Accio/Imperio charge voices and fuller Patronus body, alongside the soft additive glow and eleven-spell visual pass. The Bombarda launch fallback, three-second Accio guidance limit and earlier gameplay remain regression checks. See [VISUALS.md](VISUALS.md) for the new treatments and rendering limits. Procedures below are not native passes; evidence is recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).

## Install and launch preflight

Unload the old plugin or close Rocket League before replacing its DLL. Use the **same 0.9.2 DLL, all twelve WAVs and both PNGs on both PCs**. In the BakkesMod injector, choose **File → Open BakkesMod folder**. In Windows Explorer, verify all sixteen runtime files: `plugins/WizardRumble.dll`, `plugins/settings/wizardrumble.set`, the twelve files `avada_kedavra.wav`, `leviosa_cast.wav`, `leviosa_float.wav`, `eat_slugs.wav`, `stupefy.wav`, `petrificus_totalus.wav`, `protego.wav`, `expecto_patronum.wav`, `bombarda_maxima.wav`, `obliviate.wav`, `accio.wav` and `imperio.wav` under `data/wizardrumble/audio`, plus `glow_soft.png` and `glow_mist.png` under `data/wizardrumble/textures`. Compare all sixteen runtime files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. An installer reporting success is not enough if the game cannot see the files.

Start BakkesMod, then launch Rocket League using **Epic Library → Rocket League → three dots → Launch without EAC**. Epic documents `-noeac` under Manage/Launch options as a fallback; remove it for normal online play. Steam offers an EAC-disabled choice. Confirm the injector reports injection and **F2 opens its menu**. [Official launch instructions](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-37599050/c-42869540/a16737879).

Load `WizardRumble` through F6. For multiplayer, both PCs need NetcodePlugin ID 166, chat Allow All and no muted participants. Create a **LAN Soccar** match with **Rumble None**, join opposite teams, and record the game build, BakkesMod version, DLL SHA256, map, mutators and `wr_status` output on each PC. Require `glow_textures=1` locally on each; resources are not transferred from the host. After correcting either installed PNG, run `wr_glow_reload` outside an active cast and check readiness again.

Use `wr_cooldown 1` on Host for rapid coverage, then restore `wr_cooldown 10`. This setting affects new cooldowns. In solo Freeplay, use `wr_practice accio`, `wr_practice protego` or another retained spell identifier to choose a spell directly. Verify the practice command refuses to grant spells in LAN or ordinary online matches, and rejects `glacius`, `descendo` and `expelliarmus`.

## First: reproduce the user's LAN-bot setup

1. Create a locally hosted **LAN Soccar** match with **Rumble None**, the host as the only human and at least one opposing bot. Wait for active play.
2. Run `wr_status` in F6 and save the roster rows, bot flags and actor identities. In **F2 → Plugins → Wizard Rumble**, use **Test Leviosa on a LAN bot**, or enter `wr_bot_test leviosa` in F6. Close the console and watch the selected bot.
3. The test enables `wr_debug 1`. Record current pose/velocity/contact lines and the release reason/probe result. Leviosa must physically rise until roof contact and then fall; Petrificus must hold for two seconds after its normal charge. Zero error after a setter alone is not proof of native behavior.
4. Use **Test Petrificus on a LAN bot** or `wr_bot_test petrificus_totalus`. The bot should stop at its cast position and rotation for **two seconds**, then resume. Record `held_near_2s`, `released`, pre-correction samples and the release reason. Preserve CHECK results.
5. Repeat with several bots and rematches to catch numeric-ID changes, on moving and airborne targets, and while bots continue attempting to drive. A hold must remain attached to the same current PRI/car pair. Test goal, pause, demo, disable and unload cleanup afterward.
6. Verify the bot-test commands refuse ordinary online matches, Freeplay, inactive/disabled sessions, LAN matches with another human, and matches without an eligible opposing bot. Turn logging off with `wr_debug 0` after collecting evidence.

These local host/bot checks do not prove remote human client prediction or two-PC replication.

## 1. Spell slot and controls

1. Check the slot at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and the user's normal resolution. It should sit at the bottom-left, leaving space for the native ball-camera text and safe-area edges, without covering score or replay prompts. Check all HUD scales from 0.7 to 1.6.
2. While charging, check the radial countdown and number. The fill and number should finish together; the displayed cooldown should match the host setting.
3. Check the cleaner spell slot, distinct glyphs, name and cast-key hint at the default HUD scale. The new 20-pixel measured font height at 1080p is slightly smaller than the previous 22; names must remain readable against bright grass and dark arenas. Long names should use two lines. Check the Obliviate glyph and reduced decoration without obscuring the name.
4. Verify the cast-key hint says **R**, press R once, then mash R. One held spell should yield one gameplay effect and one cooldown. For Avada and Leviosa, one press starts the two-second charge; Eat Slugs starts a 1.5-second wind-up; Stupefy starts a 0.65-second charge; Petrificus starts a 1.5-second charge; Protego starts a one-second ball charge; Bombarda Maxima starts a two-second ball charge; Expecto Patronum starts a three-second ball charge; Obliviate starts a half-second opponent charge; Imperio starts a one-second opponent charge; Accio starts a half-second ball charge; repeated presses must not restart it or produce duplicate applications. Cooldown starts after successful application, not at charge start. Caster/target invalidation during active play keeps the unconsumed charged spell available; pause, goal and disable follow the whole-dispenser reset instead.
5. Cast with no valid target. The held spell should stay available and feedback should explain why.
6. Collect repeated random draws. Ascendio, Reparo, Glacius, Descendo and Expelliarmus must never appear. All eleven current spells, including Eat Slugs and Expecto Patronum, must be reachable.
7. Check migration from an earlier release: Q should stop casting when its old BakkesMod command was exactly `wr_cast`. An unrelated Q command must remain intact. R should cast while the plugin is loaded. Unload the plugin and verify its previous R assignment is restored, or removed if previously empty. If R was manually reassigned after load, unloading must preserve that newer assignment.
8. Repeat using the optional controller bind. Confirm one press casts once.

### Target crosshair

Before the crosshair checks, verify a fresh setup defaults to 5,500. Upgrade a saved 4,500 old-default value, enter a supported Freeplay or LAN session and verify the one-time migration changes it to 5,500. Repeat with a custom nondefault value such as 6,200 and verify it stays unchanged. Record `wr_status` fields `range` and `range_revision`; do not count a static source check as a passed native config-load test. A legacy seven-token W2 state must still mean 4,500. Check Host and Client at the new boundary, including targets between 4,500 and 5,500.

1. In Freeplay, grant Accio, Bombarda Maxima, Protego and Expecto Patronum in turn. Each ready spell should show a crosshair centered over the visible ball within range, including when using ball camera. Casting should return to cooldown and hide the crosshair.
2. Move through the host's range boundary. The indicator should appear at or inside range and disappear outside it. Change `wr_range` and repeat on Host and Client; the indicator must use the host's setting rather than the client's local value.
3. In a LAN match, aim the car's nose toward an opposing car with each of the seven opponent spells. The marked opponent should match the existing aiming cone and priority. Turning the camera alone must not select another opponent.
4. Move all opposing cars outside range or outside the aim cone. No crosshair should remain. Check that allies, your own car, spectators and demolished cars are never marked.
5. Check a target behind the camera, off-screen, very close and near each screen edge. No inverted, stretched or screen-spanning crosshair should appear. Return it to view and verify the indicator follows it normally.
6. Check cooldown, no held spell, disabled mode, pause, ended round, local spectator and waiting-for-host states. The target crosshair should be absent. During opponent charges, the reticle stays on the locked car and uses the spell-specific countdown; Protego uses its one-second countdown, Bombarda its two-second countdown and Patronum its three-second countdown on the original ball actor; it must not jump to a better-aimed car. Hide the HUD with `wr_hud 0`; restore it and confirm targeting returns. `wr_vfx 0` should not hide a valid HUD target crosshair.
7. Test 720p, 1080p, the user's normal resolution, and all HUD scales. The crosshair should stay readable without covering the target. Check the cleaner name label and ensure the boost display remains unobscured.
8. Treat the crosshair as a local range/aim preview. The host must still reject an invalid cast if the target moves or changes state before the cast arrives, keeping the caster's spell when no valid target remains.

### Accio motion and control

1. In Freeplay, grant `wr_practice accio`. Place the ball ahead, behind, to either side, near range and above the car. Cast while stationary. The ball must travel toward a point about 260 units in front of the horizontal nose direction, decelerate instead of overshooting the car, and resume normal physics after settling for 0.35 seconds.
2. Repeat while driving forward, reversing, turning and boosting. The destination should follow the caster; a settled moving ball should match the caster's motion sufficiently to leave it controllable. Release must not zero a moving car's ball speed to world zero.
3. Cast on a fast incoming or outgoing ball and under different frame rates, including a hitch. Velocity and acceleration must remain bounded; the ball must not teleport or acquire non-finite motion. The approach interval is distance-dependent, from 1.2 to 2.8 seconds, but guidance must stop at **three active seconds** even if a distant ball has not arrived. Earlier arrival or touch still releases normally. Check the tether disappears at the same deadline on Host, Client and goal replay.
4. Touch the ball during guidance with either team, interrupt using Bombarda Maxima, and send it into Protego. Ordinary play, the new impulse or the ward collision should take control; Accio must not keep pulling through it.
5. Demo/respawn the caster, replace/reset the ball, switch caster car, pause, score, disable and unload. No guidance may attach to a replacement actor or carry into another round. A later Accio replaces the existing guidance rather than creating competing controllers.
6. Repeat the successful moving-caster and interruption checks with the caster on Client. Record Host and Client motion, control after release and any rubber-banding. Pure guidance simulations do not establish native gravity compensation, collision or replication behavior.

### Avada charge, target lock and voice

1. In a hosted LAN match, draw Avada and aim at an eligible opponent. Verify the special green ready reticle. Press **R once**, release it and time the result. The charge should last two active seconds; there must be no immediate demo. A fresh accepted charge should play the installed voice clip once locally on each receiving PC with sounds enabled.
2. Mash R during the charge. It must keep the original deadline and locked target. Move the car's aim toward another eligible opponent; the charge must not retarget. At completion the host must revalidate the same current PRI/car pair, opposing team and range before one demo and one cooldown.
3. Move the locked target out of range, demo or remove either participant, replace the target car, change teams, pause, score, disable and unload. Check cancellation on both PCs, no demo of a replacement car, and retention of the held spell when the round continues. A new cast should work after a cancellation.
4. Join or reload Client during a charge. Its snapshot should show only the remaining charge and should not restart the two-second gameplay deadline or play the voice. A successful late finish must still end at the captured impact point even if the victim immediately respawns elsewhere.
5. Verify the installed paths with `wr_status`, then use **Reload spell audio files** or `wr_audio_reload` after replacing any WAV. A missing, invalid or unreadable file should log a useful error while gameplay and visuals continue. Restore the bundled WAVs and reload; no plugin restart should be required.
6. Sound playback is optional during this checklist. If desired, set `wr_sfx_volume` to a comfortable value, then voluntarily use **Test Avada voice locally** or `wr_audio_test`. Check 0, 0.25 and 1 without changing Windows or Rocket League master volume. Verify `wr_sfx 0` suppresses subsequent spell sounds independently of `wr_vfx` and `wr_hud`.
7. Test overlapping accepted Avada charges and interruption/unload while a clip is playing. Playback should remain bounded, stop for its canceled charge and not leave stale sound after cleanup. Record native device errors or audible clipping separately from static WAV measurements.

### Avada upper-view sky flash

1. Cast with level, upward, downward, ball-camera and rear-facing views in outdoor and enclosed arenas. The brief dark-green sky/atmosphere flash should follow the upper visible region and expire quickly, while the pitch, target and HUD stay readable. This is a local projected overlay, not a map sky-shader or weather change.
2. Repeat with transparent Canvas strokes forced opaque in the recording harness, and then in the native game. Sparse/dithered fallback marks must not become an opaque full-screen rectangle. Test overlapping releases, disable/unload and map transitions; no tint may persist.
3. Join during a charge or during unrelated persistent effects. Snapshots must not replay an old sky flash. Check native frame rate while lightning, wards and the camera overlay share the frame.

### Leviosa charge, roof ascent and release

For a local ascent-only check, run `wr_selftest leviosa` in Freeplay. This diagnostic bypasses normal target selection and the two-second charge. Close F6 and watch the physical car rise to the roof, release and fall. Record the `Leviosa live probe` roof/release/fall result. This does not replace a normal R-cast Host/Client test. The old `wr_lift_seconds` and `wr_lift_height` settings no longer control Leviosa and must not appear in its settings UI.

1. Cast normally on a stationary grounded opponent. There should be a two-second charge with the supplied voice and CASTING countdown, with no early lift. Afterward the car should rise continuously at 700 units/second until physical roof contact, then drop under normal gravity. There must be no fixed three-second plateau or 650-unit stop.
2. Repeat while the victim presses throttle, steering, jump and boost, with keyboard/controller and with no input. Rise and roof release must not depend on input callbacks; horizontal movement must remain controlled by the spell while rising.
3. Test flat ceilings, curved upper stadium sections, a car already near the roof, and a high/rolled car. A nearby current upper-body contact may release the car; old floor contacts, side-wall contacts and distant contacts must not release it early. Native collision must stop the car at the surface without position teleportation through it.
4. On a map with no detectable roof, verify the eight-active-second safety deadline releases the car. That deadline is a fallback, not a normal hold time. Pause time must not count into another round.
5. Observe both Host and victim Client. After release the car should fall; drive, steer, jump and boost should work again. It must never remain frozen until a goal or inherit ascent on a replacement car.
6. Chain Leviosa and Petrificus Totalus and interrupt using a demo, pause, goal, disable or unload. Release only the current owned effect. Repeat with a frame hitch and stopped input callbacks.
7. Use `wr_audio_test leviosa` and `wr_audio_test leviosa_float` for voluntary local auditions. A normal accepted G plays the voice once during charge; successful E starts the floating clip once. The three-second floating clip may finish before a longer rise ends. It must not loop or restart each frame.
8. Overlap lifts with different start/end times. Roof release of an older car must not stop a newer different target's charge, voice or floating sound, including same-ID bots.
9. Join Client during a charge and ascent. Snapshots must restore only remaining visuals and never replay speech, restart the ascent or extend the host deadline. Periodic refreshes must not restart a voice.
10. With a short cooldown, try Leviosa on a target still affected by your own active Leviosa. Keep the ready spell rather than stacking a new same-pair charge. Other targets remain eligible.

### Eat Slugs 1.5-second charge, audio, blobs and victim camera

1. In local Freeplay, use `wr_selftest eat_slugs` or its F2 button to preview the victim view. This direct diagnostic skips the 1.5-second charge and voice; test a normal R cast separately in LAN. Slugs should splat across most central vision, remain briefly, then slide down and fully clear within 3.75 active seconds. Repeat at 720p, 1080p and 1440p and check that bodies, highlights, eye stalks and slime read as slugs rather than rectangular panels.
2. In a two-PC LAN match, cast on the opposing Client, then reverse roles. Only the current victim receives the screen obstruction. Caster, uninvolved players and spectators can see the world cast but must not receive sticky screen slugs. Verify the actual PRI/car pairing, including bot matches where numeric IDs can repeat.
3. Time R, the first large dark-green blobs, impact, central coverage, lingering and final slide. Before 1.5 active seconds, no flying volley, victim overlay or cooldown consumption may occur. Successful release launches the blobs and consumes once; their short flight precedes the full 3.75-second camera effect. The volley should arrive at the same time that camera slugs begin, and must not replay a full flight during the obstruction. The victim's HUD stays readable while most driving vision is obscured; inputs retain their normal functions.
4. While affected, set `wr_hud 0`, `wr_vfx 0`, then both to zero. The vision debuff must remain until its deadline. Restore them; no unrelated old world casts may replay. Disable the mode on Host and verify the gameplay obstruction clears.
5. Join or reload the victim Client during the effect. The snapshot must enter the remaining linger/slide phase and expire at the host's deadline, rather than starting another full 3.75 seconds. A repeated hit replaces the bounded current effect without accumulating unlimited bodies.
6. Demo/respawn, replace car, switch to spectator, leave, score, pause and unload during the obstruction. No replacement car or new round may inherit the overlay. Repeat while many wards, holds and cosmetic casts are active; cosmetic budget pressure must not evict the active vision effect early.

7. Use `wr_audio_test eat_slugs` to audition the installed clip voluntarily. A normal accepted fresh charge starts it once; spamming R or joining a remaining snapshot must not replay it. It may finish naturally after release and the projectile flight. Cancellation during the charge should stop only the matching cast voice.
8. Test a target leaving range, changing team, demoing or respawning during the 1.5-second wind-up. Keep the unconsumed spell during active-play invalidation, with no camera obstruction on a replacement car. Check a same-caster/same-victim reapply while the old obstruction is active: the new charge should not start, and the ready spell should remain. Other casters and different targets stay eligible.

### Petrificus Totalus charge, audio and two-second bind

1. In LAN, aim at an eligible opponent and press R. The supplied `petrificus_totalus.wav` voice, charge energy and CASTING countdown start once. At 1.499 seconds there must be no body bind; at or after 1.5 active seconds, revalidate the same target and start its two-second pose/rotation hold. The voice must not restart at E.
2. Aim at a neighbor during the charge, move the target out of range, change team, demo/respawn or replace either car. Invalidation must cancel without binding a replacement or consuming the ready spell while active play continues. Repeat R and duplicate/snapshot G/J/P handling must not duplicate the voice or hit.
3. Hold throttle, steer, jump and boost during the bind with keyboard/controller. Verify the car stays at the cast pose for two seconds after impact, then releases without scoring. A two-second outline alone is not evidence of a physical hold.
4. For direct Freeplay diagnostics, run `wr_selftest petrificus_totalus`; for the sole-human hosted LAN-bot test use `wr_bot_test petrificus_totalus`. These direct hold tests bypass normal charge/audio. Record the near-deadline and released probe fields.
5. Repeat with a frame hitch, no controls, Leviosa chaining, demo, pause, goal, disable and unload. An older same-ID bot's cancellation must not stop a newer target's charge or audio. Replay the goal and hear this voice once at its recorded charge event; J/P must remain silent.

### Obliviate controls and restoration

1. Apply Obliviate to Host, then to Client in a separate test. Compare W with S and A with D before, during and after the five-second effect. Both acceleration and braking/reversing directions should swap.
2. Repeat with a controller: throttle/reverse triggers, steering stick, aerial pitch/yaw/roll and directional dodge input. Include partial analog input and the neutral/deadzone position; inversion must not turn a small input into full strength.
3. Jump, boost and handbrake during the effect. Their action buttons should keep their functions. Casting remains on R or the chosen optional controller bind.
4. Confirm each direction reverses once. A victim Client must not briefly predict normal steering while the Host applies reversed steering, nor cancel inversion by applying it twice.
5. Let five seconds expire without scoring. Recast during the effect, demo/respawn, switch to spectator, disconnect/rejoin, score, disable and unload. No replacement car or new round may inherit reversed controls.
6. Join late during an active effect and verify only its remaining duration is applied. Test restored transport after an interruption; stale effects must not persist indefinitely.

The effects are projected Canvas overlays. They do not use the game's full depth-buffer occlusion. Document any distracting appearance through stadium scenery; do not confuse an overlay visibility limit with a gameplay collision problem.

## 3. Protego barrier

1. Cast toward a ball moving along the ground. After the one-second voice and charge, an open circular runic shield should appear **ahead of the original ball**, facing its current travel direction, and the ball should bounce off it. Its collision radius is 350 units.
2. Repeat while the ball travels left/right, toward either goal, diagonally and nearly vertically. The wall must have a valid orientation and remain fixed where cast, rather than chasing the ball.
3. Cast with a stationary ball. The caster's forward direction should supply a stable placement/orientation.
4. Send fast balls through the wall's plane, including a fast Bombarda Maxima shot. A ball passing through the panel's area should not tunnel through between ticks.
5. Send the ball beyond the circular rim and through the empty regions that used to be rectangular corners. Missing the finite disk, allowing for the ball's own radius, should leave the ball unchanged; no invisible square or infinite wall may remain.
6. Approach from the reverse side. Check a sensible reflection and no repeated jitter, sticking or oscillation at the plane.
7. Drive a car through the panel. It should not acquire car collision or restore the old Protego immunity. Hostile spells should still affect that car normally.
8. Observe the circular rims, readable runes, open inner arcs and appearance/impact/fade animation from both PCs. There must be **no filled blue surface**. The pitch and ball must remain visible through the open areas even if individual strokes appear opaque. Check bright/dark arenas, frontal/angled cameras, and close/distant views. Visible and physical radii must match.
9. Cast another Protego while a barrier exists. The prior barrier remains active during the one-second wind-up and is replaced only on successful release; cancellation preserves it. Different casters' walls may coexist. Check the same behavior on both PCs.
10. Let the wall expire, score a goal, pause, disable the mode and unload the plugin. No invisible collision or lingering wall should remain afterward.

## 4. Host/client synchronization and recovery

1. Join opposite teams and begin active play. Each player receives their own spell after the host's cooldown. Client input must not choose arbitrary spells, targets or deadlines.
2. Check the ready glyph, cooldown duration and effect geometry on both PCs. The barrier position and orientation should match, even if the cameras differ.
3. Join late while a hold, Obliviate, wall, Eat Slugs or any of the eleven charged spells is active. Check that current effects synchronize with their remaining lifetime, without replaying unrelated old casts or charge voices. Eat Slugs must join its correct slide phase.
4. Disconnect/rejoin while held or confused. The replacement car must not inherit an old hold, outline or reversed controls. Spectators cannot cast or be targets.
5. Unload/reload Wizard Rumble on Client and verify a fresh synchronization. To reload NetcodePlugin, unload Wizard Rumble first.
6. Temporarily change chat/mute settings to interrupt the transport, then restore them. A stale local countdown must not be treated as proof that the host still accepts casts.
7. Check mixed-version behavior by confirming every participant has the new DLL before any gameplay test. Do not expect the earlier protocol to interoperate.

## 5. Cleanup, maps and local preferences

Run with Accio guidance, Avada/Leviosa/Stupefy/Petrificus/Eat Slugs/Protego/Bombarda charges, a Leviosa lift, Eat Slugs, Petrificus Totalus, Obliviate, a Protego wall and a Patronum flight active where applicable:

1. Score a goal and watch replay/kickoff. No old spell effect or collision should enter the new round; charging starts fresh.
2. Pause/unpause the locally controllable session. Temporary effects should clear safely.
3. Set `wr_enabled 0` on Host, then `1`. Gameplay effects clear, clients receive the state and a new cooldown begins.
4. Unload the host plugin. Guidance and charging stop, held cars recover, Obliviate stops changing input, wall collision ends, local audio stops and visual state clears.
5. Leave the match, rematch or load a different map. Old actor references and barriers must not affect the new world.
6. Join a saved replay or an ordinary online private match. No gameplay spell effects should be applied.
7. Hide the local HUD with `wr_hud 0`, then disable world animations with `wr_vfx 0`. Neither setting may change host physics or another player's display. An active local Eat Slugs overlay must remain despite either cosmetic preference. Test the documented independent toggles and restore them.
8. Adjust `wr_hud_scale` through 0.7, 1 and 1.6. Check legibility and no overlap at the tested resolutions.
9. Change range and cooldown settings; verify obsolete lift-height/seconds controls are absent. Host values govern gameplay; clients do not override them.

## 0.8.1 target and goal replay checks

1. In a LAN match with several opposing bots close together, start a charged spell on one bot, then point the car at its neighbor. The charge reticle, animation and final hit must stay with the original target. If that car disappears or respawns during the charge, cancel safely and keep the spell.
2. Have an older Leviosa or Eat Slugs effect expire while charging the same spell at a different bot. The new charge, voice and reticle must continue; shared numeric bot IDs must not merge the two cars. Obliviate must reverse only its actual victim's controls for its full duration.
3. Cast Leviosa. Hear the two-second voice once, with no repeating sound after the cast. At lift onset, hear the supplied floating effect once; it must not repeat. The car keeps rising until roof contact, then falls; the floating clip remains one shot.
4. Use visible spells just before scoring.
