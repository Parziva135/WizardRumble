# Octane model credit

The Expecto Patronum ghost mesh is derived from **Octane - Rocket League Car** by **Jako**.

- Author: [Jako / fairlight51](https://sketchfab.com/fairlight51)
- Original model: [Octane - Rocket League Car](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/octane-rocket-league-car-9910f0a5d158425bbc7deb60c7a81f69)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Supplied source filename: `octane_-_rocket_league_car.glb`

These title, author, source and license identifiers are carried in the supplied GLB's `asset.extras` metadata.

Wizard Rumble converts the supplied model into bounded numeric derivatives for its projected Canvas renderer: the existing contour/edge mesh in `include/PatronumMesh.h` and, since 0.9.1, **176 area-distributed luminous surface samples** in `include/PatronumVolume.h`. Surface positions and normals are sampled from the transformed source geometry, with body, glass and four wheel regions retained for a recognisable miniature silhouette. `scripts/bake-patronum-model.py` and `scripts/bake-patronum-volume.py` record those conversion procedures.

Since 0.9.2, `include/PatronumShell.h` adds two bounded filled representations generated by `scripts/bake-patronum-shell.py`. They are **stylised profile lofts**, not copies of the original triangle topology: cross-sections of the transformed body/chassis establish the exterior profile, source wheel bounds establish the four tires/hubs, and the source rear-wing profile establishes a separate wing. Dark glazing and simplified closed faces make the summon more substantial. The detailed representation contains 448 triangles; the coarse representation contains 304. The baker uses only Python's standard library and the adjacent volume baker; its original GLB input is not required to run or compile the already-generated mod.

The derivatives change the model's scale and presentation to a luminous silver/cyan summon: a filled body supplies its mass, overlapping additive soft-light sprites and sparse original-model contours supply surface light/detail, and a restrained mist trail follows its movement. These numeric representations are compiled into the DLL and distributed with this attribution. They do not use the original model textures or present the derivatives as the original model. The raw GLB is excluded from the runtime package.

`assets/textures/glow_soft.png` and `assets/textures/glow_mist.png` are original procedural light masks generated by `scripts/generate-glow-textures.py`; they are not extracted from the model or films. They supply the projected light appearance rather than a native volumetric car material.
